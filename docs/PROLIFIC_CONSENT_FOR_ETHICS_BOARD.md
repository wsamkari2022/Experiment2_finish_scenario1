# The Prolific version's consent page, for the ethics board

Written 7 October 2026; the attention checks and their rule updated 14 October 2026; the university column 16 October 2026.
The study now has two versions on one website:

- **The university version** (https://moonlander.fit.edu): FIT students and employees, who sign in with their email. Since
  16 October 2026 its consent page is word for word the Florida Tech consent submitted to the IRB with the modification of
  14 October 2026: **course credit** for CSE4301/CSE5290 instead of the $5 Amazon gift card (version
  `2026-10-16-course-credit`). It goes live only after the IRB approves the modification.
- **The Prolific version** (https://moonlander.fit.edu/prolific): Prolific participants, paid through Prolific and
  identified only by their Prolific ID. Its consent page is the same page with **eight parts changed**, because
  Prolific's rules differ (version `2026-10-14-prolific-will-be-rejected`, saved with every agreement).

**Approval is needed before the Prolific version is opened to participants:** recruitment through Prolific, the new
consent text below, storing the Prolific ID, and the attention-check rule.

**Not yet in the code (the next step):** the Prolific consent submitted with the IRB modification of 14 October 2026 adds
the list of payment rules and the privacy sentences of its Appendix 2. The Prolific column below is the page as it is in
the code today; it will be brought into line with the submitted text before the Prolific version opens.

## What stays the same in both versions

These sections have the same words in both versions: the header ("Informed Consent"), Study Title, Purpose of the Study,
What You Will Do (the five parts and the guess about the participant), the time ("at least 35 minutes"; it said "about 40
to 55 minutes" in both versions until 7 October 2026, so the university version changed here too), Possible
Risks, Possible Benefits, and Who to Contact With Questions (the researcher and the IRB).

## The attention checks in both versions (since 14 October 2026)

Every check is a **reading** check: it tells the participant exactly which answer to choose, says it is a check, and needs
nothing remembered (the advisor's request, and Prolific's rule for a valid check). None of them asks about the study.

- **Two short screens inside the study, in both versions:** "How do you usually get to work or class? This question
  checks that you are reading: please choose 'By boat', whatever your real answer is." (after Block 1, 2 or 3) and
  "Which of these do you enjoy most on a free afternoon? This question checks that you are reading: please choose
  'Reading a book', whatever your real answer is." (after scenario 1, 2 or 3). Each person's places are drawn at
  random and kept, so the checks are spread through the study, not placed at the end.
- **"Pick the number" rows in the feedback questions:** one in the university version, two in the Prolific version
  (one in "The tools & the experiment design", one in "How this experience was for you", each a different number from
  two to five), each at a random place, never first in its section.

A person is never told whether they answered right. **In the Prolific version all four checks count: failing two or
more of them means the submission is rejected** (the researcher's decision, 14 October 2026), which is what the consent
page says and what Prolific allows for a study longer than 5 minutes. Failing one is never a reason. In the university
version a check answered wrongly is recorded for the researcher only (one of the four careful-participation signs); the
course credit is given on completion (since 16 October 2026; until then all three were needed for the gift card). Before 14 October 2026 the two screens asked
what the part just finished was about, and only the two number rows counted on Prolific; nobody took part under that
version.

## Where this lives

The page is `src/experiment/ConsentPage.tsx` (the Prolific parts are the functions `ProlificSitting`, `ProlificPayment`,
`ProlificPrivacy` and `ProlificAgreement`). A check (`npm run validate:attention`, T10) holds the university version's
wording and the Prolific version's key sentences, so neither can change without the check noticing.
