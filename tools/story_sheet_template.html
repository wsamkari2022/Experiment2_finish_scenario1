Simple English Explanation

What this page is, in plain words.

In Block 5 the participant picks an option. Then one person speaks to them. We want to know whether hearing a real person changes the participant’s mind — and how close that person is to them changes how loud that voice should feel.

So every option needs two stories, because the participant can go two ways:

  1. If they say “yes, I still choose this”, they meet the person this choice hurts. We ask: knowing that, do you still choose it?
  2. If they say “no, not any more”, they meet the person who needed it. We ask: knowing that, do you go back to it?

Both stories have to be fair. If the sad story is strong and the hopeful story is weak, then everybody changes their mind, and we have measured our own writing instead of the participant. That is why the five rules above exist, and why a script now checks all sixty every time the project is built.

The one thing a script cannot judge is whether a sentence is true and worth reading. That is what this page is for.
